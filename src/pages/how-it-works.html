How it works

Five stages. Nothing hidden.

Here's what happens at each stage, who does what, and what the employer and the candidate can see along the way.

01Stage

Demand submission

The employer shares the roles, headcount, trade standards, pay, allowances, housing and target start window. We review the request and confirm what's realistic before anything begins.

EmployerSubmits the request and answers our questions
MASTurns it into a clear role brief and plan
ApplicantNothing yet. The vacancy is still being verified
02Stage

Candidate sourcing & trade testing

We find matching candidates in India, check identity, experience and credentials, and run practical trade tests where the role needs them, recorded only with the candidate's consent.

EmployerReceives progress updates
MASSources, checks documents, runs assessments
ApplicantSees the verified job details, then applies and takes the test
03Stage

Client interview & selection

The employer reviews a shortlist with the assessment evidence, interviews candidates, and makes the decision. We share only consented, relevant information.

EmployerInterviews and selects
MASArranges interviews and records the decisions
ApplicantInterviews and receives the outcome
04Stage

Visa & medical clearance

Written terms are agreed. We coordinate the work-authorisation application and any required medical checks through authorised parties. Decisions rest with the authorities, and we show the status without exposing health details.

EmployerProvides the employer-side documents
MASCoordinates the paperwork and tracks status
ApplicantSigns the terms, provides documents, attends appointments
05Stage

Final deployment

Once lawful authorisation is confirmed, travel is planned and the agreed onboarding support begins. Pre-departure orientation prepares the worker for the job and the country.

EmployerWelcomes and onboards the new team member
MASCoordinates travel, orientation and check-ins
ApplicantTravels with a signed contract and a contact for help
What employers trackPer worker
  1. Candidate shortlisting
  2. Trade test passedor not applicable
  3. Medical clearancestatus only, no health details
  4. Visa / work authorisation decision
  5. Flight booking
  6. Deployment & onboarding
What applicants trackPer application
  1. Submitted
  2. Eligibility & document review
  3. Assessment / trade testif applicable
  4. Employer interview & decision
  5. Offer & written terms
  6. Work authorisation & documents
  7. Travel readiness
  8. Deployed, or closed with a reason
