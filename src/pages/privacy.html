Privacy notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This notice explains what personal data MAS International collects through this website and through our recruitment process, why we use it, and the choices you have.

1. Who we are

MAS International is responsible for the personal data described here. You can reach us about privacy by phone or WhatsApp on +371 29 333 473, every day 9:00 a.m.–6:00 p.m.

2. What we collect

  • Employers: contact name, role, company, email, phone, and the details of your workforce request.
  • Applicants: name, mobile number, state and city, trade, experience, the documents you hold, and the languages you speak.
  • Later stages only, and only when your application moves forward: identity and travel documents, certificates, assessment results and recordings (with your consent), and medical clearance status where the law or the job requires it.

3. Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry or request, which is a step you ask us to take before any agreement.
  • To match applicants with verified vacancies and run assessments, based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • To coordinate work authorisation, travel and onboarding, and to meet our legal obligations.

4. Who we share it with

We share applicant information with prospective employers only when it is relevant to a hiring decision and you have agreed. We also share what is needed with authorised partners and authorities involved in work authorisation, and with service providers that help us run this website and our communications. We never sell personal data.

5. WhatsApp and website services

When you send a form, it opens WhatsApp with your details so that you can send them to us. Messages sent through WhatsApp are also handled by WhatsApp under its own privacy policy. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and loads fonts from Google Fonts. These providers may process your IP address to deliver the site.

6. International transfers

Recruitment between India and Europe means personal data moves between countries. When that happens, we protect it as data protection law requires.

7. How long we keep it

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as the law requires. Applicant details are deleted when they are no longer needed, or sooner if you ask us to delete them.

8. Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, object to or limit how we use it, and withdraw consent. Contact us on +371 29 333 473. You can also complain to a data protection authority, which in Latvia is the Data State Inspectorate.

9. Cookies and analytics

This site uses no tracking or advertising cookies. If we add analytics in future, we will ask for your consent first.
