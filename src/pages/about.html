About MAS International

Recruitment that's fair to both sides of the route.

Employers need people they can rely on. Workers need opportunities they can trust. We built MAS International to serve both, with clear steps, honest information and lawful routes.

How we work

Four principles behind every placement

Clarity before commitment

Employers get a written plan. Applicants get the real job, pay and contract before they decide. Nobody signs up to something vague.

Evidence over promises

We show what we checked and how. You won't find inflated numbers, borrowed logos or guarantees we can't keep on this site.

People first

Every candidate is a person moving their life to a new country. We explain every step, protect their data and give them a way to raise concerns.

Lawful routes only

We work only through proper work-authorisation routes, with authorised partners, and we follow official guidance in India and Latvia.

Compliance & responsible recruitment

Clear rules, followed openly

We follow the official rules for overseas recruitment in India and Latvia, and link to them here. If anything you hear in our name doesn't match, please tell us.
