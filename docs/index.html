Questions

Common questions

Which countries do you recruit for?
Our focus is employers in Latvia. Other European markets are added once the right authorisations and partners are in place.
How long does it take to get workers on site?
It depends on the role, the number of workers and official processing times for work authorisation, which we don't control. We give you a realistic plan for your request and keep the status updated as each stage completes.
Can I see a candidate's skills before I select them?
Yes. Shortlists include assessment notes, and for practical trades we can share recorded trade tests where the candidate has given consent. You can also interview candidates before deciding.
What does the service cost?
Pricing depends on the roles and the scope you need. We explain it in writing before you commit.
Do I have to pay to apply?
No. Applying is free. Never pay cash or unofficial charges to anyone claiming to represent MAS International.
Will I definitely get a job or a visa?
No one can honestly promise that. Selection depends on the employer, and visas and work permits are decided by the authorities. We explain your status at each stage and what is still pending.
What documents will I need?
Usually a passport, your trade certificates or licences, and proof of experience. We ask for documents only when your application moves forward, and only through a secure route.
Can I apply if I only speak Hindi?
Yes. The application is available in English and Hindi. Some jobs need basic English or another language. If so, it is written clearly in the job details.
