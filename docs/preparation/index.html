Candidate preparation

Screened for the role. Prepared for the move.

Every candidate is checked against the specific job. Selected candidates are prepared for the work, the contract and life in a new country.

Screening

Four checks, matched to the job

Which checks apply depends on the role. We record what was done and by whom, so the result means something.

Documents & eligibility

Identity, passport status, licences and certificates are checked against the role's requirements.

Practical assessment

Hands-on tests for skilled trades: welds, driving, tool use and reading drawings, scored against an agreed rubric.

Communication

A check that the candidate can follow work and safety instructions in the language the job needs.

Work readiness

Motivation, attitude and a clear understanding of the job, the contract and living conditions.
