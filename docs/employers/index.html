What we handle

Less sourcing work for your team, more confidence in who arrives

You choose the scope. Every service below is agreed in writing before work starts.

Role scoping

We turn your need into a clear brief: trade standard, experience, language, pay band, allowances, hours, contract length and start window.

Sourcing in India

We find candidates who match the brief and check their identity, experience and documents before shortlisting.

Practical assessment

Hands-on trade tests where relevant: welds, driving, tool use. With the candidate's consent, they're recorded for you to review.

Interview & selection

You get a shortlist with the evidence attached. We arrange interviews and record your decisions.

Documents & work authorisation

We coordinate contracts, the required documents and the work-authorisation steps with you, the candidate and authorised parties.

Travel & arrival

Where agreed, we coordinate flights, arrival and the first days, plus check-ins once your new team members have started.

What you see before you select

A shortlist you can actually judge

Every candidate summary shows what was checked, by whom, and when, so you can compare people on the same terms.

  • Assessment results

    Trade test outcome against an agreed rubric, with evaluator notes.

  • Recorded practical tests

    Short clips of the actual work, shared only with the candidate's consent and only with your approved team.

  • Sensitive data stays protected

    Passport and health details are never shown in shortlists. You see only what the hiring decision needs.

The secure employer portal (video library and live tracker) is being built. Until it launches, we share shortlists and updates directly with your team.

Employer FAQ

Before you request

Do you work with small headcounts?
Yes. Requests can be for one specialist or a larger team. Tell us your needs and we'll say honestly whether we're the right fit.
Who employs the worker?
This depends on the agreed model and the rules that apply to your country and sector. We explain the options and responsibilities in writing before you commit. [Owner to confirm employment model]
What if a worker isn't the right fit?
We agree the support and replacement terms in your contract up front. [Owner to set replacement policy]
How are timelines set?
We give a realistic plan based on the role, number of candidates and current official processing times. Work authorisation decisions are made by the authorities, so we never promise a fixed date we don't control.
Can I visit India for interviews or trade tests?
Yes, if it suits you. We can arrange in-person sessions, or run interviews by video if you prefer.
