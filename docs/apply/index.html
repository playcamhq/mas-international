How to apply

  1. STEP 1

    Tell us about you

    Your name, phone number and where you live.

    Your trade

  3. STEP 3

    We call you

    When a job matches, we explain it and ask for documents in a safe way.

We will never

  • Ask you to pay cash or unofficial charges to an individual
  • Ask you to travel on a tourist or visitor visa to work
  • Promise a visa, a job or a fixed income before it is confirmed
  • Ask for original passports or certificates over WhatsApp

Someone asked for money in our name? Report it here

Before you accept any job, check

  • The employer's name and the city
  • Gross pay, and what will be deducted
  • Hours, overtime and contract length
  • Who arranges housing and travel, and who pays
  • A written contract before you travel
